Kirid Support

Last updated October 8, 2026

About

Kirid is a family recipe vault that stays on this device. Recipes, photos, archive cards, and Card Credits are kept in this installation. There is no account.

In-app help

Open Settings for Shop, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Clear All Data. Legal pages open inside the app.

Purchases

Gift packs and treasure chests are Consumable In-App Purchases processed by Apple. This version does not offer Restore Purchases. Preview of archive cards does not use a Card Credit. Confirming generation uses 1 Card Credit after the PNG pages are saved on this device.

Permissions

Camera, Photos, and Microphone are requested only when you tap Scan Recipe, Import File, Save Image, or Speak Steps. The notification prompt appears once on the first Recipe Vault visit. This version does not track you and does not send notifications.

Contact

For App Store review questions, use the review notes bundled with this build, or this Support page.
